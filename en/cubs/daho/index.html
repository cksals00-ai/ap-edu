Story

The eldest of the four. He opens every episode and shows today’s letter first. Put one block under 바 and it becomes 반, then 방 — that first principle is Daho’s job.

In the app he hosts lesson 1, “One block changes everything”, the ㅁ and ㅂ bottom blocks, and everyday words like food, the house and time. On YouTube, the first hello of Lesson 1 “ㅏ” is his too.

When the younger ones scatter, he’s the one who gathers them. Low, slow voice, always leaving room for you to repeat.
