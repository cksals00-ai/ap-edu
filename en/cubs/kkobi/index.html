Story

Kkobi owns the sounds. 문 and 물, 말 and 몸 — pairs that change meaning with one bottom block, told apart at the tip of the tongue.

In the app he hosts lesson 2, “Water, doors and horses”, the ㄴ and ㄹ blocks, and words for the body, school and fruit. On YouTube he is the “word snacker” who brings each episode’s three words — ice cream, baby, daddy.

Moves the most, laughs the most. One kick, one word.
