Story

Rami holds the brush. “One long stroke down, one short stroke to the side” — on YouTube she is the one who makes you trace the letter.

In the app she hosts lesson 4, “Crisp stops”, the ㄱ and ㄷ blocks, and words for numbers, weather and things that move. 집·책·곰·밤 — sounds that cut off cleanly are her territory.

She starts the trouble, and she cheers the loudest.
