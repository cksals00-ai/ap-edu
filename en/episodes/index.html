Letter lessons

Starting with the ten vowels. One letter, three words and one writing pass per episode.

Lesson 1 · ㅏ AH

Your First Korean Letter: ㅏ (AH)

Start here. Open wide — ahh. Learn three words, follow the two writing strokes, then find the right letter. Turn on English captions to see the Korean words and the spoken instructions.

아빠 dad아기 baby아이스크림 ice cream
▶1:39
Lesson 2 · ㅑ YA

Learn Korean Vowel ㅑ (YA)

One vertical bar, two short arms. Add one arm to ㅏ and it says “ya”. Listen, repeat, write.

야구 baseball야채 vegetables야옹 meow
▶1:28
Lesson 3 · ㅓ EO

Learn Korean Vowel ㅓ (EO)

Arm to the left — “eo”. The mirror of ㅏ. Practice with 엄마, 어깨 and 어항.

엄마 mom어깨 shoulder어항 fishbowl
▶1:38
Lesson 4 · ㅕ YEO

Learn Korean Vowel ㅕ (YEO)

One more arm on ㅓ — “yeo”. Say 여우, 여름 and 여덟, write ㅕ, then a short review.

여우 fox여름 summer여덟 eight
▶1:32
Lesson 5 · ㅗ O

Learn Korean Vowel ㅗ (O)

Round the mouth — “o”. A short stroke on top of a horizontal line. 오리, 오이, 오렌지.

오리 duck오이 cucumber오렌지 orange
