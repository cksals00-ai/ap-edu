30-Day Challenge · November

30 days of Korean with the cubs. Meta AI glasses for one of you.

Learn with the app and the videos through November, post your review on our board and on your own social channel — and one learner, chosen by review, gets Ray-Ban Meta AI glasses. Any country.

    30 days. One board post a week.

    Board review + your own social post, both.

  4. Results2026-12-12

    One winner + five encouragement prizes. Shipped in December.

What to do

Both the board review and your own social post are required to be judged.

  • 4 weekly logs

    On the board, tagged #challenge W1–W4: one letter you learned, a screenshot of the app’s Review tab, one line.

  • 3+ episodes

    Three of YouTube Lessons 1–5. A one-line comment is enough to show it.

  • Final review — board

    300+ characters or a 60-second video. Any language.

  • Final review — your channel

    A public post on your blog, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or Facebook, tagged #HangeulCubs, with the link added to your board review.
